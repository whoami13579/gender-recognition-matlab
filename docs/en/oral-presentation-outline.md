# Oral presentation outline

The Traditional Chinese draft has 20 editable slides. The language, slide count and rehearsal allocation are team choices. The December 15, 2026 date and coverage come from the course requirements.

| Slides | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4 | Title and team placeholders, course context, high-dimensional input, four methods, seven metrics, training and reserved test profiles |
| 5–6 | System flow and fitted input representation, from RGB to a 4,096-dimensional row |
| 7–10 | Economy SVD PCA, regularized low-rank Fisher LDA, Gaussian log-posterior odds, and a tanh MLP with one logit |
| 11–12 | Subject-grouped outer folds, isolated inner selection, full-training recommendation |
| 13–15 | Baseline and nested accuracy/AUC and overlapping mean ROC, model counts and verified timing scopes |
| 16–17 | Frozen configurations, independent status and actual MATLAB execution |
| 18–20 | Method trade-offs and limitations, provisional training-side choice, sources |

Allow roughly 2 minutes for slides 1–4, 4 for methods, 4 for the experiments and final models, 2 for the live demo, and 2 for the conclusion and references during a first rehearsal. Measure the delivery and adjust after the actual time allowance is known. Keep formulas beyond those shown, the full tie-break order, serialized sizes and per-fold plots for questions if time is short.
